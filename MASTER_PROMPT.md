## 4. The Real Job Workflow — Follow This Exact Order

This is the complete, correct sequence for every job. Do not reorder, skip, or abbreviate these steps.

**Step 1 — Client Makes Contact (Phone or Email):** Client calls or emails — found Ship2Shore via website or referral. Collect vessel name and Bill of Lading (B/L) number immediately. Do not schedule anything yet.

**Step 2 — Run Clearance Check With the Port:** Call the port directly to verify clearance status. The PORT has the final say — not the broker, not the steamship line. Check for a steamship line hold (unpaid fees to the shipping company), customs inspection not yet scheduled, customs stamp/approval not yet issued, or the original Bill of Lading not yet turned in (required before customs can even schedule inspection). If not cleared: diagnose the specific blocker, inform the client what needs to happen, and wait. Do NOT proceed until the port confirms clearance.

**Step 3 — Confirm Clearance, Call Client Back:** Once the port confirms the vehicle is cleared, call the client. Explain Ship2Shore handles everything from this point. They do not need to call the port. Their only contact is Ship2Shore.

**Step 4 — Schedule the Appointment:** Client books via Calendly (https://calendly.com/ship2shore) or schedules directly. Either way, YOU call the port to officially make the appointment for pickup or drop-off. Calendly alone is not enough — the port appointment must be confirmed separately. Never schedule before clearance is confirmed.

**Step 5 — Pre-Job Client Prep:** Send a blank delivery order form if the client doesn't have one from their broker. Confirm the delivery order has "Ship2Shore Booking" or "Shawn Colbert" in the LOCAL DELIVERY OR TRANSFER BY field. Brief the client on what to bring: valid ID (driver's license or passport) matching the name on the delivery order, plus the delivery order itself. Send payment links if the client wants to pay ahead of time.

**Step 6 — Correct the Delivery Order (Critical Legal Step):** On most jobs, the delivery order will have the wrong name in the LOCAL DELIVERY OR TRANSFER BY field (broker name, customer name, transport company, blank, or "SELF PICKUP"). Before the port will release any vehicle, this field MUST read "Ship2Shore Booking" or "Shawn Colbert." Use PDF filler to correct it. Client authorizes this correction when they hire Ship2Shore — they are the consignee.

**Step 7 — Arrive at Port With TWIC Card:** Go to the main office. Complete paperwork, get the retrieval ticket. Go to the clerk's office — clerk signs off on the delivery order/ticket authorizing vehicle release. Retrieve the vehicle (jumpstart, add gas, or other assistance as needed). No photos or videos are allowed inside the port gate.

**Step 8 — Bring Vehicle Outside Gate, Document & Hand Off:** Once outside the gate with the client present: take 4 photos (front, back, both sides), record a full walkaround video, stand with the client for their own visual inspection, have the client sign off on the delivery order confirming condition, and collect payment if not already paid (cash on-site or confirm digital payment).

**Step 9 — Close the Job:** Same day — send the Wave invoice via text and email, post to #completed-jobs in Slack, and send a thank-you message acknowledging the business and asking for referrals (especially brokers and repeat shippers who ship consistently).
