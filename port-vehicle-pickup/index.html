Port Vehicle Pickup — Long Beach & Wilmington

Professional Port Extraction & Terminal Coordination

Retrieving a vehicle from a marine terminal requires precise timing, verified paperwork, and authorized pier access. We coordinate directly with steamship lines and terminal clerks to confirm your paperwork and gate pass requirements with the terminal, and pull your vehicle the moment it is released.

Terminals We Access

  • Port of Long Beach (Matson Auto & Oversized Cargo Yard on Pier C St, ITS Pier G, TTI Pier T, LBCT Pier E)
  • Wilmington (Port of Los Angeles Harbor Terminals)

Condition Documentation

Every vehicle pulled by Ship2Shore undergoes an immediate digital inspection (recording six standard condition photos). You receive a complete condition report with photos taken right at the dock before it moves an inch.
