How an imported car gets from the port to you

Most cars imported from Japan, including JDM trucks, vans and sports cars, arrive in Long Beach on a roll-on/roll-off ship or in a container. Your importer or customs broker handles the import paperwork. After that the car has to be released by the port and picked up by someone who is allowed inside the terminal, and that is the part we do.

  1. Your car arrives and clears

    Your importer or customs broker clears the car with customs and the shipping line. The port cannot release a vehicle that has not cleared.

  2. Send us the details

    Vessel name, bill of lading number, VIN and a signed Delivery Order. Your Delivery Order must name Ship2Shore Booking in the "Local Delivery or Transfer By" box, and we correct that field when you hire us.

  3. We book the appointment

    We schedule the pickup with the port and give the 24 hours' notice Wilmington and Long Beach require. At Matson we get a booking number first.

  4. We go in and get the car

    Our TWIC-credentialed escort enters the secured terminal and brings your car to the gate. If it will not start we jump start it, add air and provide two gallons of gas if needed.

  5. We document it

    Photos, and video when required, with a condition report you sign at handoff.

  6. Get it home

    Drive it away from the gate, or let us connect you with a licensed carrier for a longer move.

Imported car pickup FAQ

How do I pick up an imported car from the port?

Once the port shows your car released, send us the vessel name, bill of lading number and VIN, plus a signed Delivery Order that names Ship2Shore Booking. Our TWIC-credentialed escort goes in, brings the car to the gate and completes a condition report with you. You do not need a TWIC card and you do not go inside.

How much does it cost?

The TWIC vehicle escort is $95 per vehicle, with no hidden fees. Military and PCS orders are $80. If the car needs to be moved after pickup, the carrier gives you its own price.

What usually delays an imported car?

The most common delays are a missing original bill of lading or an unpaid shipping-line balance. Check those two first. The port cannot release a car that has not cleared, and we verify the steamship line, customs and gate-pass requirements before we go.

Who handles the import paperwork and rules?

Your importer or customs broker handles the import paperwork and any rules about bringing your car into the country. Ship2Shore handles the pickup at the port once the car is released.

Can you move the car to my home or shop?

If it needs to go farther, we connect you with a licensed, insured auto carrier we work with. The carrier sets its own price, signs its own agreement with you and operates under its own motor carrier authority. Ship2Shore does not set carrier prices or take payment for their haul.

Do I need an appointment?

Port of Wilmington needs 24 hours' notice for TWIC escort jobs, and Port of Long Beach is an appointment pier where we also give 24 hours' notice, and Matson pickups run on a booking number we get from Matson.

Which days are the ports open?

Port of Wilmington is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and closed Wednesdays. Port of Long Beach and Matson Terminal (Long Beach) are open Monday through Friday. All are closed on weekends. Hours can change, and pickup depends on your car and paperwork being cleared before pickup.
