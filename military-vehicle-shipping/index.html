Picking up your car from the port on PCS orders

Marine terminals are closed to the public. To walk into the secured area you need a TWIC card (Transportation Worker Identification Credential), and most service members don't have one. We do. You don't need a TWIC, and you don't go inside: we find your vehicle, bring it to the gate and hand it off to you.

Wilmington is a frequent stop for military personally owned vehicle pickups. We also work at Port of Long Beach and Matson.

How it works

  1. Book a time

    Choose a pickup time on our booking page, or call. We need 24 hours' notice for Long Beach and Wilmington.

  2. Send your paperwork

    Bill of Lading number, vessel name, VIN and a signed Delivery Order. We send you the authorization form when you book.

  3. We check that it's cleared

    Before we go, we verify the steamship line, customs and gate-pass requirements. The port cannot release a vehicle that hasn't cleared.

  4. We pick up your vehicle

    We enter the secured port, locate your vehicle and bring it to the gate. If it won't start we jump start it, add air and provide two gallons of gas if needed.

  5. We document it

    Condition photos, and video when required, with a report you sign at handoff.

  6. Get it home

    Drive it from the gate yourself, ask us about delivery, or let us connect you with a licensed carrier for a longer move.

Delivery and longer moves after pickup

Pickup is only half of a PCS move. For deliveries we handle ourselves we can take your vehicle to your home, your base or a hotel. If it has to go to a new duty station farther away, we connect you with a licensed, insured carrier we work with. The carrier prices the move, signs its own agreement with you and runs it under its own motor carrier authority, so your contract for the long haul is with the carrier.

Tell us where it needs to go and when, and we will point you to the right carrier.

Military car shipping FAQ

How do I pick up my car from the port on PCS orders?

Book a TWIC escort with Ship2Shore. You do not need a TWIC card and you do not go inside. We send you a Delivery Order authorization when you book, go into the secured port with your Bill of Lading number, vessel name and VIN, find your vehicle and meet you at the gate.

How much does it cost for military members?

The TWIC escort for military and PCS orders is $80 per vehicle, $15 off the regular $95 rate. There are no hidden fees. Moving the car after pickup is priced separately, and you see the price before you agree to anything.

Who qualifies for the military rate?

Active duty military, veterans and personnel on PCS orders. Tell us when you book.

Can you deliver my car to my home or base?

Yes, for deliveries we handle ourselves we can take it door to door, or hand the vehicle to you at the port gate. Ask for a delivery price when you book.

Can you ship my car to another state?

If your car needs to go farther, we connect you with a licensed, insured auto carrier we work with. The carrier sets its own price, signs its own agreement with you and operates under its own motor carrier authority. Ship2Shore does not set carrier prices or take payment for their haul.

What do I need to bring or send?

Your Bill of Lading (B/L) number, vessel name, VIN and a signed Delivery Order authorizing Ship2Shore to pick up the vehicle on your behalf. Keep a copy of your orders handy in case we need to confirm the military rate.

Which ports do you cover?

Port of Long Beach, Port of Wilmington (Port of Los Angeles) and Matson terminals. Long Beach and Matson are open Monday through Friday. Wilmington is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. We need 24 hours' notice for Long Beach and Wilmington. Port hours can change, and pickup depends on your vehicle being cleared before pickup.
