Matson Vehicle Pickup

Matson pickups run on a booking number. We call Matson with your vehicle’s information to get one, then schedule your pickup appointment. You don’t need a TWIC card and you don’t go inside: our TWIC-credentialed escort enters, locates the vehicle and brings it to the gate.

How pickup works at Matson

  1. Send us your paperwork

    Send the items listed below. Your Delivery Order must name Ship2Shore Booking in the “Local Delivery or Transfer By” box.

  2. We call Matson

    We call Matson with your vehicle’s information to get a booking number.

  3. We schedule the appointment

    With the booking number, we schedule your pickup appointment.

  4. Your escort finds the vehicle

    The TWIC-credentialed escort goes in, locates your vehicle and brings it to the gate. A jump start, air and two gallons of gas are included if needed so it can leave.

  5. Condition report and handoff

    A condition report is completed at the gate, with photos and video when required, and signed at handoff.

Days and notice

We pick up at Matson Monday to Friday. All ports are closed on weekends.

The appointment can only be set after Matson gives us a booking number, so send your paperwork as early as you can, then call (310) 748-0040 if you have a date in mind.

Port and terminal hours can change. Availability depends on the port’s schedule and on whether your vehicle and paperwork are cleared before pickup.

What you need to send us

  • Delivery Order
  • Vessel name
  • Bill of Lading number
  • VIN

Your Delivery Order must name Ship2Shore Booking in the “Local Delivery or Transfer By” box.

Need your vehicle moved after pickup? Call us and we'll connect you with a carrier.

Questions

What is the booking number?

It is the number Matson gives us when we call with your vehicle’s information. We use it to schedule your pickup appointment.

Do I need a TWIC card?

No. You never need a TWIC card and you do not go inside. Our TWIC-credentialed escort enters for you.

Is Matson open on weekends?

No. We pick up Monday to Friday, and all ports are closed on weekends.

What if the vehicle is electric or needs more than a jump start?

Electric and hybrid vehicles, and anything more involved, go to the port’s professional mechanic. A jump start, air and two gallons of gas are included if needed.
