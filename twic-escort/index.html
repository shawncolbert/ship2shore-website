Why you need a TWIC escort to pick up a vehicle at the port

Federal security regulations prohibit general public access to marine container terminals. If you imported a vehicle, bought a JDM import, or are trying to retrieve a car from the port, a TWIC holder has to go in for you. A TWIC (Transportation Worker Identification Credential) is the federal ID required to enter secured port facilities, and most vehicle owners, dealers and even many truckers don't have one.

That is the job we do. You don't need a TWIC card, and you don't go inside. We handle the pier and meet you at the gate.

What your TWIC escort does

  • Gets through port security and works with terminal management, so you never call the port.
  • Finds your vehicle inside the secured terminal and brings it to the gate.
  • Gets it moving. If it won't start or is low on air or fuel, we jump start it, add air and provide two gallons of gas if needed.
  • Documents the condition at the gate with photos, and video when required, and a condition report you sign at handoff.
  • Fixes the Delivery Order field that stops most releases. The port won't release a car unless "Local Delivery or Transfer By" reads Ship2Shore Booking, and we correct it when you hire us.

How a TWIC escort pickup works

  1. Book a time

    Choose a pickup time on our booking page. We need 24 hours' notice for Long Beach and Wilmington.

    We need your Delivery Order, vessel name, Bill of Lading number and VIN. We send you the authorization form when you book.

  4. We go in and get your vehicle

    We enter the secured port, locate your vehicle and bring it to the gate.

    Condition photos, and video when required, with a report you sign at handoff.

TWIC escort FAQ

How much does a TWIC escort cost?

A TWIC vehicle escort (pickup or extraction) is $95 per vehicle. Military and PCS orders are $80 per vehicle. Hotshot port service is from $200 and heavy equipment handling is $325. There are no hidden fees, and same-day jobs use the same rates.

Do I need my own TWIC card?

No. A TWIC (Transportation Worker Identification Credential) is a federal ID required to enter secured port facilities. We are TWIC-credentialed, so we go in for you, find your vehicle and meet you at the gate.

Can I come into the port with you?

No. Port security requires a valid TWIC card to enter secured areas. We handle everything inside and meet you at the gate.

What do you need from me?

Your Bill of Lading (B/L) number, vessel name, VIN, and a signed Delivery Order authorizing Ship2Shore to pick up the vehicle on your behalf. We send you the form as soon as you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Port of Long Beach is an appointment pier. We give the port 24 hours' advance notice before every pickup and drop-off, and we schedule the appointment directly with the port. Port of Wilmington also needs 24 hours' notice for TWIC escort jobs.

Which days are the ports open?

Port of Long Beach and Matson Terminal (Long Beach) are open Monday through Friday. Port of Wilmington is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and closed Wednesdays. All three are closed on weekends. Port and terminal hours can change, and pickup depends on the port's operating schedule and on your vehicle and paperwork being cleared before pickup.

What if my vehicle won't start?

If your vehicle won't start or is low on air or fuel, we'll jump start it, add air, and provide two gallons of gas with the service if needed, so it can leave the port. Electric and hybrid vehicles, and anything more involved, are handled by a professional mechanic at the port.

Ready to get your vehicle out of the port?

Your only contact is Ship2Shore. You don't call the port and you don't chase a broker.
