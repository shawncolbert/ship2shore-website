TWIC card at Long Beach: do you need one to pick up your car?

By Shawn Colbert, TWIC-credentialed port escort · Updated October 5, 2026

You can't just walk into the port and drive your car out. The terminals are secured federal facilities, and a TWIC card is required to enter on your own. If you don't have one, you have two options: get a TWIC card, or have a TWIC-credentialed escort go in for you. Either way, your vehicle has to clear four checks first. Here's how it works at the Port of Long Beach and the Port of Wilmington.

How a port pickup works

  1. Check that your vehicle is actually cleared

    Every vehicle at the port is held by one of four things. A broker saying "it's cleared" isn't enough, because the port is the authority.

    • Steamship line: no unpaid balance owed to the shipping line.
    • Customs: inspection scheduled and completed, and the clearance stamp issued.
    • Bill of lading: the original has been submitted. Customs can't schedule an inspection without it.
    • Gate pass: once the terminal operator issues the gate pass, your vehicle is cleared for release.

  2. Get the right paperwork

    You need your bill of lading number, the vessel name, your VIN and a delivery order. The delivery order has a field called "Local Delivery or Transfer By." The port won't release the vehicle unless that field names the party actually picking it up.

    The most common delay

    Many delivery orders arrive blank, or with a broker's name, the owner's name, or "Self Pickup." None of those work for an escorted release. When you hire Ship2Shore Booking, we correct the field to read Ship2Shore Booking, so there is no extra paperwork on your end.

  3. Book your pickup with 24 hours' notice

    Long Beach and Wilmington both need 24 hours' advance notice. Same-day pickups are sometimes possible, so call to check. Wilmington is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and closed Wednesdays and weekends. Long Beach and Matson are open Monday-Friday and closed weekends.

  4. Get your vehicle out of the port

    Without a TWIC card you can't enter the secured area. A TWIC-credentialed escort goes in with your paperwork, locates the vehicle, gets the clerk's sign-off and brings it out to the gate. If it needs a jump start or a little gas, the escort can usually take care of that too.

  5. Inspect it at the gate

    Outside the gate, with you there, we take photos of all four sides and a walkaround video. You look the vehicle over and sign off on the delivery order confirming its condition.

Should you get a TWIC card instead?

A TWIC card is issued by the Transportation Security Administration. It involves an in-person enrollment, a background check, a fee and a wait for the card to be issued, and the TSA decides who qualifies. You can read the requirements on the TSA's TWIC page. Even with a card, you still need the cleared vehicle, the correct delivery order and a port appointment.

If you work at the ports regularly, a TWIC card makes sense. For a single car or truck pickup, most people find it simpler to have an escort handle it.

Importing a JDM truck or sports car?

The process is the same: clearance, delivery order, appointment, escort. The most common delay we see is a missing original bill of lading or an unpaid shipping-line balance, so check those two things first.

Questions people ask

Do I need a TWIC card to pick up my car from the Port of Long Beach?

You need a TWIC card to enter the secured terminal area on your own. If you don't have one, a TWIC-credentialed escort can go in with your paperwork, get the vehicle released and meet you at the gate. Ship2Shore Booking does this for $95 per vehicle.

Can I come into the port with the escort?

No. Port security requires a valid TWIC card for entry into secured areas, so the escort goes in and meets you at the gate to hand over the vehicle.

Why does my delivery order need to say Ship2Shore Booking?

The port won't release a vehicle unless the "Local Delivery or Transfer By" field names the party actually picking it up. When you hire Ship2Shore Booking, we correct that field to read Ship2Shore Booking.

Is Port of Wilmington open on Wednesdays?

No. Port of Wilmington (California) is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and closed Wednesdays and weekends. Port of Long Beach and Port of Matson are open Monday-Friday and closed weekends.

Are TWIC escorts still allowed at Long Beach?

Yes. TWIC escorts are allowed at the Port of Long Beach. We give the port 24 hours' notice for pickups and drop-offs, and same-day pickups are sometimes possible. Call or text (310) 748-0040 to check availability.

How much notice do I need to give?

We give the port 24 hours' advance notice before every pickup and drop-off at Long Beach and Wilmington. Same-day pickups are sometimes possible, so call to check.
